# Community Conduct

dsh-community welcomes technical contributions, bug reports, compatibility
evidence, plugin submissions, and review from anyone willing to work within the
project's documented boundaries.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence, risks, and trade-offs rather than attacking people.
- Keep reviews specific and reproducible: point to files, commands, logs,
  contracts, or documented behavior.
- Be clear about uncertainty. Do not present an unrun test, inferred result, or
  README claim as verified evidence.
- Respect contributor privacy. Remove credentials, private data, customer data,
  and unrelated personal information from issues, logs, screenshots, and test
  fixtures.
- Report suspected vulnerabilities privately through the process in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in a public issue.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately disrupt other
  contributors.

## Technical disagreement

Disagreement is expected in a compatibility and security-oriented project.
When maintainers and contributors disagree, the deciding factors are:

1. reproducible behavior;
2. the official DeepSeek Harness contract and published Runtime;
3. this repository's documented architecture/security boundaries;
4. the smallest change that preserves those boundaries.

Status, seniority, affiliation, or volume of comments do not substitute for
evidence.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, release
discussions, and other project-managed collaboration spaces.

The primary maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that
contain harassment, exposed secrets/private data, deliberate spam, or repeated
disruption after a clear warning. Serious or repeated violations may result in
participation restrictions.

Good-faith technical mistakes, failed experiments, and rejected patches are not
conduct violations.
